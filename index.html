01 / A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

The girl behind the goggles.

I believe the best kind of engineering starts with "what if?"

✦ ─────── ✦

I'm an electrical engineer interested in what happens when good ideas leave the page and become something real. I love the problem-solving, the prototypes, the inevitable troubleshooting, and that moment when everything finally comes together.

My work has taken me from testing LED products to exploring robotic systems for lunar-dust research at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center. Along the way, I've found that I love both the tiny details of embedded systems and the big-picture thinking behind entire engineering projects.

Outside the lab, you'll probably find me reading, thinking about space, or admiring the wonderfully complicated people in a Jane Austen novel.
