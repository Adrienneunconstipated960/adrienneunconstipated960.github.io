FEATURED PROJECT • ROBOTICS / CONTROLS

6-DOF Robotic Arm for High-Vacuum Dust Mitigation

Designing and simulating a ceiling-mounted robotic arm for dust mitigation experiments in a 36-inch high-vacuum chamber. The project combines kinematics, actuator control, mechanical constraints, and trajectory iteration.

  • Developing Python-based arm control with a Pololu Maestro servo controller.
  • Refining joint geometry and motion profiles to navigate chamber constraints.
  • Exploring ROS / ROS 2 integration for a more modular robotics workflow.
